You read documents.

<!-- part: pair_example -->
if the document reports one scenario for 2030, 2050 and 2100 and a second one only for 2050, that is four pairs and not six.

<!-- part: raw_word -->
the name the document itself uses ("Baseline", "Forecast", "Option B").

<!-- part: quote_why -->
 Two separate pieces of evidence, because a table header carries the years and the figure caption carries the scenario.

<!-- part: year_rules -->
Which year is a reference year: a column heading of a results table, a target year in the text ("by 2050", "in 2100"), a base year ("relative to 2010", "base year 2015").

What is NOT a reference year: the publication year, a year in a bibliographic reference, the date of a law or agreement ("Data Protection Act 2018"), a version designation and a number in a unit of measurement.
